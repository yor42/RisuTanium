### MC-4390 — #816 ChroMemory vectors persist in the byte store and ride the .rtb as one optional side row, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (session, Tier 2, critical contract). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#816`, `MC-223`, `MC-350`, `MC-774`, `MC-3074`.

**What was decided.** f32 vectors are saved per chat under `derived/chromemory-vectors/<seg>/` in the page's byte store (`head` written last as commit point, chunks `c<n>`, each record at most 4 MiB, per-unit text hash). The `.rtb` carries them as a new optional side row `side.chromemory-vectors`, omitted when empty so existing goldens stay byte-identical. No `.rtb` version bump, no new setting, no store2 change. The `.bin` export omits them structurally and its pre-export warning names the omission when the engine is on or vectors are saved (MC-223).
