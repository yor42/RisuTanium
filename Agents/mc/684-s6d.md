### MC-4865 — #684 S6d: a malformed regex flag skips only its own entry, 2026-11-28

- Tag: decision (maintainer ruling through AskUserQuestion). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Related: `#684`, `S6_REGEX_SPEC.md` sections 6.1 and 6.2, `MC-3991`, `MC-3992`, `MC-3999`, `MC-011`.

**What was decided.** The chat regex engine and the translation engine now take their flags from one shared helper. An entry whose custom flag is not text (only possible from an unchecked import) is logged and skipped by itself; before, the chat engine rejected the whole regex pass and the translator threw. Every string or absent flag behaves byte for byte as before, pinned by tests recorded green on the unmodified code first. The streaming copy of the flag rules stays as it is (K9).

**Why.** One malformed entry should not stop every regex of a pass. Nothing persisted changes: no format, no migration. Studio only validates and shows a problems line and Try it; neither writes.
