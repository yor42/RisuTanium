### MC-4810 — #149: plan for preset field locks written; the maintainer's questions are Q1, Q1b and Q2 to Q8, 2026-11-28

- Tag: plan (design defaults, not maintainer decisions). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Related: `#149`, `#148`, `#150`, `#156`, `MC-175`, `MC-223`, `MC-350`, `MC-774`, `MC-4047`.

**What was found.** The locks already live outside presets, as three top-level settings (`doNotChangeSeperateModels`, `doNotChangeFallbackModels`, `disableSeperateParameterChangeOnPresetChange`), all upstream fields. The switch side skips the locked group; the save side erases the aux models and overwrites the fallbacks and separate parameters in the slot. #150 (autosave) would make that happen on every edit.

**What the plan proposes.** `docs/design/settings/PRESET_FIELD_LOCKS_PLAN.md`: lock = skip on switch and skip on save, held as a settings field; storage option A (three booleans stay, one new fork-only `presetFieldLocks` list for new groups, left out of `.bin` with a pre-export warning). Open questions Q1, Q1b and Q2 to Q8 are for lane A to ask; Q3 is a persisted-format choice.

### MC-4811 — #149: field-lock scope and semantics, 2026-11-28

- Tag: decisions (maintainer rulings relayed by lane A). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Related: `#149`, `MC-4810`, `PRESET_FIELD_LOCKS_PLAN.md` section 10.

**What the maintainer chose.** Q1: the lockable groups are the three legacy locks plus `sampling` and `reasoning`, as group locks. Q1b: `models` and `contextLimits` are deferred. Q2: a locked group's slot keeps its own value on switch-away (the save side stops erasing or overwriting it). Q8: a Studio edit of an inactive preset's locked field writes that preset's slot.
