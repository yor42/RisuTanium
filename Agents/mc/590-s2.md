### MC-4233 — #590 S2: the publish checklist is the session's written list, and a later desktop-only release hides Android updates, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (session, within `MC-3722` and the S1 handoff; no new trade-off). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#590`, `MC-3574`, `MC-3722`.

**What was decided.** The S1 handoff names a "publish checklist, items 7..13" for the `release-desktop.yml` header, but no such list exists in the repository, in PR #642 or in issue #590 (checked by the Gate 1 reviewer). The header carries a numbered list written from the S1 hand steps and `MC-3722` instead; the maintainer can reword it.

This matches "Android off behaves as today" (`MC-3574`) and needs no guard.
