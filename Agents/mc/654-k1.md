# #654 K1 (Origin Navy, Office Sheet, colour-ready join) maintainer decisions

Block MC-5110..5119, assigned by lane A. Asked 2026-11-28 by the K1 session through AskUserQuestion, on the join plan's open items Q1, Q2, Q4 and Q5.

- **MC-5110** (Q1, escape route). Under a Settings pack sheet, the Settings > Display "Theme pack" row stays legible because an app-owned rule in `settings-pack-base.css`, scoped to area=settings, gives `theme-pack-row` an opaque scheme background. The rule is inert with no pack on. Rejected options: masking the Settings sheet on the Display page, and re-pointing `--color-*` on the Settings root (which would reverse MC-5031).
- **MC-5111** (Q2, popups base). Popups sit on the pack base over every colour-ready area, chat included. The maintainer accepted that popups change visibly for people already using a chat pack. Rejected options: non-chat areas only, and no popups base.
- **MC-5112** (Q4, ToastHost). ToastHost keeps reading the chat role tokens. Only the shared-token consumer scan pattern is fixed (MC-5035 leftover), so toasts look the same as before. Rejected option: moving ToastHost to `--overlay-*`, which would change the no-pack paint.
- **MC-5113** (Q5, Office Sheet chat). The bundled Office Sheet gets its chat sheet rebuilt as part of the full-spec pack, along with the other areas. The downloadable `theme-packs/office-sheet.risutheme` row that asserts its chat sheet is identical to the built-in is narrowed accordingly; MC-4706 coexistence is unchanged. Rejected option: keeping today's chat sheet.
- **MC-5114** (Gate 1 follow-up to MC-5110, Q3). Widens MC-5110. One app-owned rule in `settings-pack-base.css`, under area=settings, gives every scheme container hosted in the Settings shell an opaque scheme background: the Theme pack row, the custom-component rows and the legacy page bodies. Under a pack these show as scheme-coloured panels on the pack page. The rule is inert with no pack on. This replaces the alternative of recording unreadable scheme-hosted controls as a K1 known limitation.
- **MC-5115** (Q8, popups tone under a light host). Under a light pack base, popups take light overlay pins. A test must prove those light popups stay readable. If a pack's popups sheet fails that check, its popup colours are withheld and only its shape applies. Evidence for the choice: the O-3 survey found no `.overlay-host` child that mixes scheme utilities with `--nexus-*` or `--color-surface-*` reads, except ToastHost, which reads chat roles (MC-5112). Rejected options: light pins with no proof, and a dark-only overlay base.
