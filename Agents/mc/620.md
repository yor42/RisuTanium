# #620 warm workspace delete decisions

Block MC-4440..4449 (lane A brief 2026-11-27); MC-4440, MC-4441 and MC-4442 are this PR's so far. Lane A folds these into Maintainer-Context.

### MC-4440 — #620: the PC does not reproduce the warm delete; the Note 9 leg is gated on lane A, 2026-11-27

- Tag: measurement, no product change. Date: 2026-11-27. Related: `MC-2394`, `MC-1666`.

**Decision.** Phase 1 reproduced the import session on the PC (Node-server store2 page, measurement production build, four 22.9 MiB workspaces) and measured a warm delete at 0.6 to 0.9 s against 1.6 s for the first save after a fresh boot, so no stage that grows with session age exists on the PC. The 15 to 17 s of Note 9 run D-1 is attributed, unconfirmed, to a commit still in flight behind an import burst (import commits 6 to 11 s on the phone against 0.4 to 0.5 s here). The Note 9 leg (rows DV-620-1..3, meter build, `android-build` then `note9` lease) starts only after lane A's go. Report: `Agents/Findings-2026-11-27-620-warm-delete.md`.

**Consequence.** `Refs #620` stays until a Note 9 row names the stage or shows the latency vanish on an idle store. No product code is written before then.

### MC-4441 — #620: tier of a fix, 2026-11-27

- Tag: gates. Date: 2026-11-27. Related: `MC-2746`, `MC-4440`.

**Decision.** If the Note 9 confirms an in-flight-commit backlog, the preferred outcome is no code (document the latency, Tier 0). A save-loop or commit change that stops a small commit queuing behind a bulk one touches commit order and durability: a critical contract, Tier 2 with Opus Gates. A pending-save indicator would be Tier 1 and a product choice for the maintainer.

**Consequence.** Phase 2 does not start without lane A's go, and a Tier 2 route needs a designer plan and an independent Gate 1 before any implementation.

### MC-4442 — #620: the Note 9 does not reproduce the warm delete either, 2026-11-27

- Tag: measurement, no product change. Date: 2026-11-27. Related: `MC-4440`, `MC-4441`, `MC-1666`.

**Decision.** Rows DV-620-1..3 ran on Note 9 phone 1 under lease `r620-dev` with a debug measurement APK and synthetic data: three 25.7 MB imports committed in 2.5 to 3.1 s, a delete tapped 1.8 s into an import commit finished 2.1 s after the call (0.6 s of waiting for that commit), a delete after 60 s quiet took 0.8 to 1.3 s, and a delete right after a fresh boot took 1.3 to 3.1 s. The 15 to 17 s of D-1 did not reproduce. A delete does queue behind an in-flight commit; D-1 may have had a commit of 6 to 11 s in flight on the real library, which was not tested.

**Consequence.** No code. Proposed disposition: close #620 as not reproducible, with the evidence in `Agents/Findings-2026-11-27-620-warm-delete.md` (a delete waiting behind a large in-flight commit is expected latency, Tier 0). A pending-save indicator stays a Tier 1 product choice the maintainer may ask for separately. Closing the issue is asked by lane A.
