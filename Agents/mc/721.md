### MC-4323 — #721 S0b on the Note 9: a paced guest transport keeps the gap low, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions and facts (maintainer chose "Probe the paced variant" via AskUserQuestion after S0; measurement on phone 1, n=5 per arm, same library and APK as MC-4322). Date: 2026-11-27. Related: `#721`, `MC-4320`, `MC-4322`, `MC-3777`.

**What the maintainer chose.** "Probe the paced variant (Recommended)": the guest takes its call-time snapshot synchronously and posts the pieces spaced out in time. The alternatives were stopping and reporting to lane A, and building only the plain burst with a waiver of MC-3777's limit.

**Result.** Median DV-599-3 gap: one message (A) 3009 ms; plain burst (B) 1746 ms; paced with a timer yield (D1) 573 ms; paced with a message-channel yield (D2) 568 ms. Both paced arms are 19% of A. The guest's synchronous clone took 543-640 ms, the floor of the gap, and the guest wall time was about 12.1 s for D1 and 2.6 s for D2. The write lands later by that wall time, and the guest holds a second copy until the pieces are posted.

### MC-4324 — #721 edits made while a paced plugin write is in transit are overwritten when it lands, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (maintainer via AskUserQuestion in the #721 session). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Related: `#721`, `MC-3702`, `MC-4323`.

**What the maintainer chose.** "Accept and name it (Recommended)". A plugin write over the size threshold now takes about 2.6 s to cross the bridge in paced pieces while the page stays responsive, and it takes its MC-3702 turn when its last piece (END) arrives. A user edit or another plugin's write made in that window is overwritten by the plugin's call-time snapshot, where today the page is frozen for about 3 s and nothing can be edited. The PR names it and a test pins the arrival order. The alternative, reserving the turn when the transfer starts, was rejected because a plugin could then stall every other plugin's writes.
