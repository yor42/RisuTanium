### MC-5040 — #930 chat-open prefetch of the ChroMemory persisted vectors, 2026-11-28

- Tag: records (ticket session; no new maintainer choice). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#930`, `#885`, `MC-350`.

`DefaultChatScreen` runs one effect per open chat: when the engine is ChroMemory in inject mode and the retrieval is hybrid, `prefetchChroMemoryVectors` builds the chat's `DenseStore` through the same `denseStoreFor` a send uses (same chat key, persist target and embedder model key) and so starts the persisted read; the send finds that store and waits on its `ready`. A send that follows a settled prefetch skips the 1000 ms wait; one that follows a pending prefetch waits only for the remainder. Nothing starts for a lexical chat, a chat without a persist target, or an embedder that is absent (a local model on a phone) or throws. "Head exists" is not checked separately: the loader reads the head first and an absent head leaves an empty store, which costs one small read.

Release: a store opened by a prefetch and not yet used by a send is marked `prefetchOnly`; the effect cleanup (chat switch, settings change, screen teardown) disposes it, and a send clears the mark, so a store a send used lives under the existing two-chat LRU cap. A read still in flight at the dispose imports nothing (`importLoaded` ignores a disposed store). Memory: the prefetch holds at most the same vectors a send would load (about 30 MB per chat on the Note 9, PSS +98.6 MiB seen in #885); the two-chat cap and the release bound it. Read path, persisted bytes and the loader are untouched.
