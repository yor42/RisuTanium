### MC-4240 — #751 Theme packs may use bounded effects through a fork-side admission pass, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (ticket session, inside the #751 brief). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#751`, `MC-2942`, `MC-3010`, `MC-3422`, `MC-3638`, `MC-3121`.

**What was decided.** Pack validation admits transitions, transforms, animations, filter and backdrop-filter through an explicit allow-list with bounds (`src/ts/themePack/effects.ts`). The pass runs inside `judgeSheet` before the reference lint, claims only declarations and top-level `@keyframes pack-<area>-*` blocks that pass every check, and blanks them from the text the reference lint sees. Anything it does not claim keeps its legacy refusal, so existing packs and current refusals stay valid. No blanket pattern is admitted. z-index, fixed and sticky stay banned (MC-3422).

### MC-4241 — #751 The reduced-motion fallback is an app-appended OS-media tail in the pack's own style elements, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (ticket session). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#751`, `MC-3010`, `MC-4240`.

**What was decided.** MC-3010's mechanism does not exist for packs, so the app appends verbatim copies of each judged selector entry inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)`, with `!important` and durations collapsed to 0.01 ms (not `none`, so `animationend` still fires). Every selector carrying an animation also goes into the transparency group, which covers keyframe filters. The tail lives only in the pack's own `style[data-theme-pack]` elements, so safe start and turning the pack off remove it. A throw while building the tail fails closed to "no sheet". The tail follows the OS only; there is no `html.rm`, `rm-off` or low-power variant (blocked on OQ-2).

### MC-4242 — #751 The effect bounds are designer defaults, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (ticket session, designer plan 3, Gate 1 r3 [EDITORIAL]). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#751`, `MC-4240`.

**What was decided.** All values are literal. Transitions up to 1000 ms plus 500 ms delay. Animations are 170 ms × the number of frames, up to 20 s, 1–10 iterations or infinite, same-sheet keyframes only (16 blocks of 16 offsets, 4 KiB). translate 64 px or 100%, scale 0.5–1.5, blur 8 px, backdrop blur 24 px. These are the designer's defaults, open to the maintainer's change at any time; a change edits constants in `effects.ts` and the tests that name them.

### MC-4247 — #751 Known limitations accepted under MC-3638, 2026-11-27

- Tag: records (ticket session). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#751`, `MC-3638`, `MC-3121`.

None breaks one of MC-3638's five invariants (code run, network request, write outside the pack's records, unbounded intake, safe-start failure). translate px and % budgets are independent. An empty keyframes block gives N=0 and a floor of 0. App `animationend`/`transitionend` listeners may see bursts under reduced motion (the toast div has no timer fallback and no data-part). A paint transition on outline-color or box-shadow can delay a focus indication up to 1.5 s. Up to three animations can combine past the flash floor. Backdrop brightness/contrast on a reach part can lower text contrast. Blur on non-chat screens takes effect only where they are colour-ready (MC-3121).
