### MC-5003 - #278 A .bin asset is skipped on exact length and bytes, not a stored checksum, 2026-11-28

- Tag: decisions (session, Tier 2, Gate 1 r3 and Gate 2 accepted). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#278`, `#224`, `MC-5002`, `MC-175`, `MC-223`.

**What was decided.** A `.bin` entry header carries no checksum, so "length plus checksum" (MC-5002) is read as the held value having the entry's length and exactly its bytes, compared piece by piece in `src/ts/drive/binAssetHeld.ts`. Every doubt writes; a held-side failure writes; a failure reading the backup file stops the import. Only the first entry of a case-folded key is compared. The design row's "shared helper with `targetHolds`" and "no IPC send" are not met: the compare is its own module over injected store functions.
