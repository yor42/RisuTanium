### MC-4370 — #770 The usage telemetry unions tools over all blocks of a provider message and binds the call to its first turn, 2026-11-27

- Tag: decisions (session, Tier 1 under the ticket's own scope: standalone analytical tooling, no persisted app format, no operational authorization, no change to `.claude/workflow-policy.json` or `Agents/Workflow-State.json`). Date: 2026-11-27. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#770`.

**What was decided.** `Agents/Tools/usage/mainloop.mjs` counts the unique tool ids of every block of one message/request tuple, including usage-free blocks, deduplicates identical usage once and keeps the call on the turn that was open at the tuple's first event. A frozen input is named by a manifest (UTC `[since, until)`, SHA-256, bytes, parser version) and a changed or missing byte is rejected. The analytical output is byte-deterministic; capture time and runtime go in a separate envelope. Diagnostics are allowlisted codes, `src-NNN` aliases and line ordinals only. The `legacy-first-block` option reproduces the superseded attribution for before/after runs.

**Evidence.** `mainloop.test.mjs` (25 tests, node vitest run) includes a source mutant that drops tools once a tuple carries usage: it loses the text-to-tool tool while call and token totals stay equal. The known-wrong 342/2.1% notification metric stays untrusted.

**Owed.** A bounded frozen real sample before/after and the usage and coverage gap export need a maintainer-approved named input; neither is claimed here.
