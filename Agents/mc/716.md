### MC-4400 — #716: an over-cap restore point gives way first, 2026-11-28

- Tag: decisions (maintainer, AskUserQuestion in the #716 session). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Related: `#716`, `MC-4052`, `MC-4051`, `MC-1119`, `DD-100`.

**What was asked.** When one Studio owner's history does not fit its 2 MiB share, what is dropped first: the restore point (deleted items and newest steps kept) or, as before, everything newer than a large restore point.

**What the maintainer chose.** "Restore point first (Recommended)". The keep walk takes deleted items, then undone and done steps (newest first), then the restore point last; the restore point is stored only when it fits in what remains. The alternative was keeping today's restore-first order.

**Consequence.** No stored-format change: the same keys, index and header are written, only the choice of which entries are kept differs, so data written by older builds reads as before (MC-350). A character with a very large alternate greetings list may lose "Restore to when Studio opened" after a reload, but keeps its recent undo steps and deleted greetings. A single step or deleted item that alone passes the owner share still drops everything after it; that is not changed here.
