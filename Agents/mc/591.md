### MC-4270 — #591 Home backdrop scrim (`bg-bgcolor/90` on `home-landing`), 2026-11-27

Sizing decision for issue #591: when `DBState.db.customBackground` has length `>= 2` (matching `getCustomBackground`'s sentinel guard so `'-'` does not trigger it) or `DBState.db.theme === 'waifu'`, `[data-part="home-landing"]` in `src/lib/UI/Home/HomeShell.svelte` carries `data-backdrop=""` and `bg-bgcolor/90` (using only the existing `bgcolor` / `--risu-theme-bgcolor` token).

Alternatives rejected:
- Deriving text colour from the backdrop image: requires async pixel sampling during Home render (conflicting with Home boot measurement in #508), fails on multi-tone images, and overrides scheme/theme-pack text colours where they are already readable.
- Per-text-block scrims: transparent Home text spans `HomeTopBar`, `HomeGreeting`, `HomeStoryShelf`, the library heading, `GridCatalog`/`CharacterSimpleList`, and `MainMenu`/`CommunityList`; a single scrim on `home-landing` covers all of them with no new `data-part`, no persisted setting, and no change to `src/ts/themePack/**`.

Device acceptance (Note 9 `home-500` row) remains owed and is not claimed by the unit contrast fixture.
