### MC-4781 — #883 A long press on message prose opens the message sheet; selection by double-tap and from the sheet, 2026-11-28

- Tag: decisions (maintainer ruling, Tier 1). Date: 2026-11-28. Exact times: UNKNOWN. Sweep ref: none. Related: `#883`, `#861`, `MC-4780`.

**What was decided.** Maintainer ruling of 2026-11-28: a long press on message prose opens the message sheet as #861 intended; text selection stays reachable by double-tap and from the sheet; a hold on a fence with a language keeps its own Copy/Download menu. `ShellMessage.svelte` sets `data-hold-select="none"` (CSS `user-select: none`, `-webkit-touch-callout: none`) on the row from a touch or pen press that arms the sheet's long-press timer until the finger lifts or the gesture is cancelled, so the WebView does not start its native selection and swallow the hold. A press that follows a quick tap (under 350 ms, within 30 px) leaves selection on, so double-tap still selects a word. A press on a fence with a language arms no timer and sets nothing. The sheet's Select text action now selects the message body's contents (it only focused the body before). No persisted format or save path changes.

**Evidence.** `ShellMessage.proseHold.svelte.test.ts` (jsdom, 4 tests): the two reproducers (selection off during the hold and back on at lift; off again after an unrelated earlier tap) fail on the origin/main `ShellMessage.svelte` and pass after; the double-tap and fence tests are compatibility guards that pass before and after. `ShellMessage.codeBlockMenu` and `.gestures` tests still pass. jsdom cannot show the WebView's native selection, so the real-hold result is owed: **Note 9 row owed** (release APK, synthetic chat): a real `adb shell input swipe x y x y 700` hold on a paragraph, on a user and an assistant message, opens the sheet with no native toolbar; a double-tap on a word selects it; Select text in the sheet selects the message; a hold on a fence with a language opens only Copy/Download.
