# Theme-ready mockups

Standing rule from 2026-10-27 ([DD-142](DECISIONS.md)). Source: the maintainer's theme-pack direction, relayed by Lane A (MC-689, MC-691, MC-692, MC-695). Reference, read-only: [Agents/Theme-Packs.md](../../Agents/Theme-Packs.md) (the 17 ideas, the capability matrix and the pack rules).

The mockups here are **Deep Nexus**, the baseline look that theme packs are defined against. A pack is tokens plus part-scoped CSS, with no free CSS. Packs change appearance, never behaviour.

## Rules for every mockup

1. **Tokens only.** Every colour, font, corner radius, spacing value, shadow, blur/transparency, motion value (duration and easing) and geometry value (box sizes, offsets, transforms, touch targets, gradient stops) comes from a named CSS custom property ([DD-143](DECISIONS.md)). A literal value in a component rule is a defect, except for 0, 1px hairlines and percentages used for layout math.
2. **Stable part names.** The main surfaces carry a `data-part="<area>-<name>"` attribute that a pack can target, for example `chat-header`, `chat-message`, `chat-message-body`, `chat-composer` and `settings-row`. Names describe a role, not a look. They don't change once published. Keep the list in the screen's spec. State a pack may style (first or last in a run, names hidden, touch or hover input and the like) is mirrored as `data-*` attributes on the part or the root, not only as classes ([DD-144](DECISIONS.md)).
3. **Critical surfaces take tokens only.** Packs may not restyle the structure of warnings, data-loss and save-failure prompts, permission dialogs, or restore and export dialogs. Mark them `data-part-critical`.
4. **Fallbacks.** Anything a pack could blur or make transparent needs a reduced-effects path; reduced motion already applies. Packs cannot blur, filter, animate or transform anything yet: the validator refuses those properties (see "What a pack covers today"), so no pack-level fallback exists. The fallback rule of MC-3010 is built together with those effects in #751 (MC-4149).
5. **Behaviour is fixed.** Focus order, keyboard paths, roles and labels must not depend on a pack's layout. Visual reordering keeps DOM order.

## Acceptance re-skins (rough, per finished screen)

- **Origin Navy:** tokens only, no part CSS. A nod to upstream's navy look. If the screen can't be recoloured without editing it, a token is missing.
- **Banana Cocoa:** the chat screen as a messenger layout (yellow bubbles on blue, own messages on the right). Part CSS may rearrange layout. If the parts can't be rearranged, the structure is too rigid.
- **Scratch White** (`scratch-white`, light only): a school notebook. Paper `#fdfdfa`, graphite text, a blue-ink accent, a red margin line down the chat transcript, one faint blue rule under every chat message and dashed perforations on the chat header and composer. The built-in sheet is a chat sheet only (it has no Studio, Settings or Home sheet); a downloadable pack can add those, but only its shapes apply there (colours apply on the chat screen only, MC-3121). The built-in names no font, so message text keeps the user's own font. The downloadable pack can add Nanum Pen Script, bundled with its OFL text; it has no paper texture yet, because the validator refuses an image on any chat surface that can hold a protected element.
- **Office Sheet** (`office-sheet`, light only): a classic document, a nod to word processors in general with no product name, logo or trade dress. Grey chrome, the chat transcript as a white page with a hairline edge and a 1 px shadow, a ruler of tick marks along the bottom of the chat header, a toolbar-style composer and the platform serif (`ui-serif, serif`) for message text. The ruler ticks are decoration, not a UI component.

These are rough probes of the contract, not finished packs. Put them under `<area>/themes/` as an override stylesheet loaded by `?theme=origin-navy|banana-cocoa|scratch-white|office-sheet`, plus stills at 360 and 1280. Record every token or part name the screen had to gain.

What the two light packs were drawn around, and what the validator does now (checked 2026-11-24):
- A repeating gradient is judged as its plain gradient, and `background-size`, `background-position` and `background-repeat` are allowed on a part (a background on a selector that names no part is still refused). The two light packs were drawn before that, so they use plain colours, borders and one non-repeating gradient.
- No image on any surface that can hold a protected element, so no paper texture there. Only small safe leaf parts accept one. A `::before` or `::after` on a part can draw a bundled image.
- `mask`, `clip-path`, `border-image` and `::before` or `::after` are allowed now. The two light packs were drawn before that, so they do not use them. On a reach part, `mask` and `clip-path` get a note: they can hide a control or clip away its tap area. A pseudo-element must come right after a part selector (for example `[data-part="chat-header"]::after`). Its `content` may only be empty (`""` or `''`).
- A chat shadow reaches at most 64 px past its element, so the Office Sheet page shadow could be softer now. The built-in sheet keeps its short 1 px shadow.
- A `max-width` of 320 px (or 20 rem) or more, or 50% or more, is accepted without a note; a smaller one installs with a layout note. The Office Sheet page can therefore be narrower than the transcript and sit centred like a print page.

Pack loading, the theme picker, bundled fonts and images are built (install from file under Display settings). Finished packs are not: the four built-in packs are chat-only and are rebuilt in #654 (MC-3871), where the other acceptance packs (Terran Eclipse and the rest) are also written.

## What a pack covers today

Status 2026-11-24 (#438, MC-4162). Every statement was read from `src/ts/themePack` and `src/lang/en.ts` on that date. This section describes the build; the sections above and below are the design contract.

**Area sheets.** A pack holds up to five area sheets that are judged and applied. Any other `<name>.css` (for example `sidebar.css`) is kept with the pack, never judged and never applied, and the install result names it. A pack needs at least one screen sheet (`chat.css`, `studio.css`, `settings.css` or `home.css`); a pack with only `popups.css` is refused (MC-3123). `sidebar.css` stays a skipped sheet and no "needs chat.css" refusal exists (MC-4161); the chat sidebar belongs to the chat sheet.

| Sheet | Screen it themes | Colours | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chat.css` | the new chat screen, with its sidebar | applied (colour-ready) | only while the chat preview switch is on, with the standard chat theme |
| `studio.css` | Studio | held, shape applied | only while the Studio preview switch is on |
| `settings.css` | the new Settings screen | held, shape applied | only while the Settings preview switch is on |
| `home.css` | Home | held, shape applied | whenever Home is on screen (no switch) |
| `popups.css` | popups, toasts and dialogs over a themed screen | colour over chat in colour mode, shape otherwise | with the screen it sits over |

**Colours.** Only chat is colour-ready. Studio, Settings, Home and popups apply the shape part of their sheet (geometry, type, layout); the colours stay accepted and kept with the pack, and the install result says so (MC-3121, MC-3125). A pack may also give default colours for chat: they appear as the pack's scheme tile (MC-2938). That needs `--nexus-canvas`, `--nexus-surface` and `--nexus-text` each set in an unconditional `:root` (or `html`) rule of `chat.css` that resolves to a concrete colour; without them the pack offers no default colours (`packColours.ts`).

**Popups, toasts and dialogs.** `popups.css` sets no custom property (MC-3007). It is on only over a screen whose own sheet applies something: the colour variant over chat in colour mode, the shape variant otherwise (MC-3126). It is never on over a screen that has no sheet in the pack, the legacy screens, Welcome and the boot screens (MC-3009). It never reaches the escape route or the protected parts of critical prompts (MC-3009, MC-3420). Notes on `popups.css` never switch the app's own colours on (MC-3660).

**When a pack shows.** The new chat screen, Settings and Studio show a pack only while their own preview switches are on (chat also needs the standard chat theme); Home whenever it is on screen, with no switch. The classic screens, group chats and the playground keep the built-in look. #438's acceptance therefore reads "when the new screens are on" (MC-4162); making the new screens the default belongs to the release-switch ticket.

**Safe start.** The Android launcher long-press shortcut "Start without theme pack" (intent action `io.github.yor42.risutanium.action.START_WITHOUT_THEME_PACK`) starts one session without the pack. A banner "Theme pack is off for this session" then offers "Turn off theme pack" and "Dismiss"; choosing None under Theme pack also turns it off. The Theme pack settings route and the banner never take pack CSS (MC-3420). Under a pack, data-loss prompts and the save-failure notice are best-effort, not guaranteed (MC-3639); the install warning says so (MC-3641).

**Installing.** Display settings has "Install from file", which opens the Android file picker; a pack saved in Downloads installs (device row DV-P, MC-4147). The install risk warning shows at every install from a file (MC-3641). The result lines are "Installed ...", "Installed with warnings (sheet)" or "Notes (sheet)", the held-colours line ("This version uses only the shapes from ... their colours are kept with the pack for a later version") and the skipped-files line ("This pack also has ... kept with the pack").

**Limits that matter to authors.**
- Properties are an allowlist (`scope.ts` `PROP_ALLOW`): colour and paint, background, border and radius, font and text, box and layout (display, position, size, margin, padding, flex, grid, overflow), outline, shadows, `clip-path`, `mask`, `border-image`, empty `::before` / `::after` content and `scroll-snap-type` (MC-4146). Anything else is refused. The effects in the next list are refused.
- On size tokens that carry a floor or a ceiling, only px, rem, ms, s and plain numbers are judged; em, vw, vh, % and ch are refused there (`engine.ts`). Use px or rem for lengths elsewhere too; other units are not verified for every property.
- Custom properties are the area's own tokens and your own `--pack-*` helpers (popups: none).
- Font families you register must start with `pack-`. Fonts and images are the files bundled in the pack; a `url()` must name a file in the pack, so nothing is fetched from a network (the install refuses a target that is not a file in the pack).
- Each sheet may name only the parts of its own area. A selector must end in a `[data-part="..."]` of that area.

**Chat sidebar.** The sidebar is part of the chat screen and takes `chat.css`; there is no separate sheet. Its formerly unmarked elements (heading, "New chat" button and menu, sort label, More menu header and items, row-menu items, chat-row lines, search results, folder rows) now carry chat parts (MC-4161, stage W2). A few small pieces (label texts, the recent strip's avatar ring, the sort wrapper) were left without a part on purpose and are styled only through their ancestors and the shared shape tokens.

**Not covered yet (planned, not built).**
- Effects: transitions, transforms, animations and `@keyframes`, filters and blur, and layering (`z-index`, fixed and sticky position) are refused. They are planned as ticket #751 (MC-4149), before the effect-based packs of #654. Blur-based looks and the reduced-effects fallback (MC-3010) therefore do not exist yet.
- Overlays without theme parts are planned for stage W3 (MC-4160, MC-4164, MC-4165, MC-4166); until then only the shared shape tokens reach them. The base list is 11 App-level overlays: BookmarkList, MemoryModalHost and MemoryModal, HypaV3Progress, EasyPanel, PopupEditor, LoadoutModal, IrisModal, CustomSidebarConfig, botpreset, listedPersona, and RealmPopUp with RealmFrame. PopupList is exempt, because only the legacy chat reaches it. W3 also covers the overlays found below App level (PartialEditController with its four modals, ModelList and OpenrouterProviderList, RealmBlockedCreators, the HypaV3 sub-modals), the code-block context menu (chat parts and theme colours instead of hard-coded gray, a lane A ruling, MC-4166) and SavePopupIcon (its save-problem buttons are critical, tokens only). PromptDiffModal gets parts on its frame, header and close button only. The CBS autocomplete popover in the shared text area is excluded (MC-4165).
- The four built-in packs are chat-only; their rebuild is #654 (MC-3871).

## Contract refinements (2026-10-27)

- **Role tokens are the contract** ([DD-148](DECISIONS.md)). Packs set role tokens, e.g. `--message-gap`, `--header-height`, `--composer-field-height`, `--sheet-padding-x`; the full list is in each area's THEME_AUDIT.md. The ranked ladder (`--space-N`, `--size-N`, `--offset-N`) is internal and may be renumbered.
- **One meaning per name** ([DD-145](DECISIONS.md)). A shared token name means the same thing with the same Deep Nexus default on every screen. Shared names live in `theme/nexus-tokens.css`; a screen-only value gets a screen-prefixed name.
- **Channel tokens for tints** ([DD-146](DECISIONS.md)). Tinted colours use `rgb(var(--nexus-<name>-rgb) / <alpha>)`, so a pack sets each colour once. Solids derive from their channel too, e.g. `--nexus-accent: rgb(var(--nexus-accent-rgb))` ([DD-158](DECISIONS.md)).
- **One layout and input attribute set** ([DD-147](DECISIONS.md)): `html[data-layout=wide|mid|phone]` and `html[data-input=touch|hover]`. Packs may set tokens under these root attributes, as well as `:root` ([DD-159](DECISIONS.md)).
- **Protected surfaces are enforced at install** ([DD-149](DECISIONS.md)). A pack selector that can match inside `[data-part-critical]` is rejected, descendants included. Protected children have their own part names. A prompt is protected when the census marks it critical (data loss, save failure, restore, export, permission; MC-2947). A menu item that leads to such a prompt, for example the row-menu delete, is an ordinary part that a pack may restyle; the prompt it opens stays protected and keeps its confirm (MC-3638). Reach is judged by ancestry: a rule whose subject may be a protected element, one of its ancestors or one of its children is checked against a declaration allowlist after escapes are normalised. Layout-breaking padding, margin, animation and flex changes fail ([DD-155](DECISIONS.md)).
- **The install rule** ([DD-242](DECISIONS.md), [DD-243](DECISIONS.md); validator `chat/checks/th/packlint.mjs` with `chat/checks/th/failclosed.mjs`, regression suite `theme/checks/run.mjs`) is judged over the worst case of every declaration a pack makes (`:root`, a root attribute, any `@media` block, any `var()` chain). Since MC-3421 and MC-3423, a finding tagged `contrast`, `readability` or `layout` is a note: the pack installs and the note shows under the "Installed" line (`failclosed.ts`). An untagged finding refuses the pack. Besides the items under "Refused" below, the property allowlist, the effects, z-index and overlay bans, the 64 px caps, unbounded sizes, and cascade and structure errors refuse too.

  Refused:
  1. **Role tokens only.** A pack may set a custom property only if its registry lists it as pack-settable: the shared colour and role names (`--nexus-*`, `--color-*`, the shared decoration hooks), the area's role tokens, and tokens the area deliberately bounds with a floor and a ceiling. Anything else is rejected with "not a pack-settable token": the internal ladders (`--space-N`, `--size-N`, `--studio-sz-*`, `--studio-sp-*`, `--studio-font-*`, `--home-space-*`, ...), `--risu-animation-speed`, and a pack's own private variables (`--banana-yellow`, `--bc-*`, `--msg-*`). **One exception (DD-258):** a pack may keep private helper variables under the prefix `--pack-` (rename `--bc-*`, `--banana-*`, `--msg-*` to `--pack-bc-*` and so on). The validator inlines them before judging: every `var(--pack-x)` becomes each value the variable has anywhere in the pack (`:root`, a root attribute, an `@media` block, a chain of other private variables) and the stylesheet is judged once per combination, so a private variable cannot carry a value past a floor, a ceiling, a colour rule, a contrast pair or an allowlist.
  2. **Colours resolve.** Every value of a colour or channel token must resolve to a concrete sRGB colour: hex, `rgb()`, `hsl()`, a named colour, or `color-mix()` in `srgb`, `srgb-linear` or `oklab`. `color-mix()` in any other space, `oklch()`, `lab()`, `hwb()`, `light-dark()`, relative colours, `currentcolor`, `inherit`, unknown functions and an unresolved `var()` are rejected, whatever the token. A channel is three numbers from 0 to 255.
  3. **A selector that names no part cannot paint or resize** (DD-242, DD-265). `[data-kind=...]` and `[data-state=...]` can match a protected element itself, so such a selector on a reach-tier element may not set a background (`background`, `background-color`, `background-image`, `-size`, `-position`, `-repeat`), `padding`, a border or border width, `font-size`, `width`, `min-width` or `max-width`.
  4. **Key controls are in the reach tier** (DD-243): navigation, Tasks, Stop, the composer with its field and Send, Apply, Undo and Redo, and the failed-save chip with its Retry. A pack may recolour, reborder and respace them; it may not hide, fade, move or re-lay them out.
  5. **Units on limited tokens.** Tokens that carry a floor or a ceiling are judged only with px, rem, ms, s or plain numbers. A value in em, vw, vh, % or ch, or one that cannot be resolved to a number, refuses the pack. A number below a floor or above a ceiling is only a note.

  Notes (the pack installs):
  1. **A fully transparent colour, a protected surface (the sheet and toast colours and each area's listed surface tokens) that is not opaque, and every contrast finding.** Text, secondary and muted text, danger, warning, on-accent, diff and ring tokens that critical parts read are checked for 4.5:1 (3:1 for non-text) against the sheet or toast they are drawn on; a control that paints its own fill is judged as a pair (text on fill, 4.5:1). A miss is a note (MC-3421). The two canvas blooms and an area's listed decorative tokens may be switched off.
  2. **A pack may set an outline, but not hide the focus ring.** `outline` and `outline-*` are allowed, up to 64 px. A rule in a focus state that makes the outline none, hidden, zero wide or fully transparent, or makes a `box-shadow` or border none or fully transparent, installs with a `readability` note. The ring tokens keep a floor (`--nexus-focus-ring-width` 1 px).
  3. **Sizes** (DD-260, DD-256, MC-3423, MC-3638). Minimum font size, touch targets, widths, a token below its floor or above its ceiling and the fit estimate are notes, as is padding above 64 px or 20% on a safe part. The root of a protected sheet (`chat-sheet`, `studio-sheet-root`, `home-dialog`) keeps the page's width: a width on it other than `auto`, `none` or `100%` is a layout note.
  Other safe parts keep the freedom DD-155 gave them: a pack may still hide a decorative banner or a problems list.
- **No size floor** ([DD-151](DECISIONS.md)). Packs may set touch-target and text-size tokens below 44 dp. Deep Nexus and the acceptance packs keep 44/48 dp.
- **Extended state and parts** ([DD-153](DECISIONS.md)). Streaming, selected, hidden-from-AI, bookmarked, current and unread states are `data-*`. Buttons, fields, composer innards, header title, list-row innards and sheet header/handle have parts. Selection, scrollbar, caret and icon size/stroke have tokens.
- **Selection colour:** Deep Nexus uses an accent-tint text selection, the one deliberate change to the default look ([DD-156](DECISIONS.md)).
- **Origin Navy** uses upstream's default scheme colours, adjusted for AA ([DD-150](DECISIONS.md), Settings DS-076). Banana Cocoa puts own-message actions beside the bubble on hover input and above it on touch ([DD-152](DECISIONS.md)).

## Colours that need !important

On every part of the reach tier (item 4 of the install rule above: the chat parts in `REACH_CHAT` and the Studio parts in `STUDIO_REACH`), a text colour or a fill must be written with `!important`. The app's own styles for these controls can outrank a plain pack rule. The app's install check refuses a plain one and asks for `!important` (the reference lint does not apply this rule):

- **Text colour:** `color`.
- **Fill:** `background`, `background-color` and `background-image`.

This holds for every value, keywords such as `inherit`, `unset`, `transparent` and `none` included, and in every `@media` block and state. Write `color: #191919 !important`, not `color: #191919`. An `!important` colour is then judged like any other, so it still needs its contrast against the fill it sits on. Other properties on these parts, and colours on every other part, take plain declarations.
