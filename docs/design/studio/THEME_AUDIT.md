## Install rule: registry, floors, ceilings

`th4/packlint-studio.mjs` keeps using chat's `lintPack` **unedited** and builds its registry from the slice 4 lists plus the three parts registries: **51 critical, 390 safe parts**, 338 default tokens, 9 contrast surfaces (new: text on the Undo toast, accent on the Undo toast, danger on the sheet), 46 floors and 40 ceilings (DD-182/192/195/197 plus this pass: `--studio-size-touch-sm` 28 / `-touch-lg` 32, `--studio-dialog-width` 240 to 720, `--studio-menu-width` 200 to 480, `--studio-toast-max-width` 200 to 720, `--studio-header-height` 32 to 96, `--studio-sp-10px` / `-sp-14px` 4 to 16 px (the ceilings are what the sheet's padding reads: a 32 px value pushed Cancel and OK off a 412 x 800 screen, which the DD-195 page check caught). ## Open (questions with a recommendation first)

1. **Menu items that delete or revert are ordinary sheet items.** The delete item in the item and card menus and the revert item in the focus-mode menu are plain `studio-sheet-item`s of the shared menu; the result is covered by the critical Undo toast, and revert by one history step. Recommended: leave them. Alternative: let `openMenu` take a `critical` flag per item and make that sheet critical (then every item of that menu becomes `studio-critical-action`). Not done because it makes the whole menu protected.
2. **Which long-text actions deserve a protected mark.** Only focus-mode revert and replace all are critical. Recommended: keep. If more caution is wanted, promote the one-click problem fix and replace one first (each needs a part name and makes its row an unsafe ancestor).
