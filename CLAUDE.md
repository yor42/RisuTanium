Maintainer interaction rules (2026-11-04, MC-1069; they override any earlier habit of asking in prose):
1. Ask every maintainer decision, approval and confirmation with the AskUserQuestion tool, never as a prose question: it is faster and it sends a mobile push notification. This covers product decisions, scope changes, and the confirmation of every commit, push and merge.
2. Once the maintainer has approved a ticket or task (chosen, ordered or queued it), proceed on your own through Gate 2 (sizing, plan, Gate 1 and its revisions, implementation in a separate worktree where needed, tests, mutants, Gate 2 and remediation). Do not ask for approval between those steps. Stop and ask with AskUserQuestion only for: a missing product decision, an escalation the rules require, a change of scope, and any commit, push, merge or other outward action.
3. Whenever other open tickets could run in parallel with the current work (disjoint files and resources, already authorized or ready to size), present them to the maintainer as options with AskUserQuestion (multiSelect) instead of choosing silently or idling.
